## About Trilles AI

> *"Intelligent systems should be structured, transparent, and dependable."*

**Trilles AI** operates at the intersection of advanced automation and enterprise reliability.

We design **AI CMO systems** that give growing businesses the operational infrastructure of a full marketing and sales department — without the headcount.

Most SMEs don't have a lead problem. They have a **systems problem**.  
Leads fall through the cracks. Follow-ups happen too late. Teams spend hours on work that should take minutes.

That's the exact problem we solve — by building agentic AI systems that handle the full revenue operations loop: attract, qualify, follow up, convert, and retain — autonomously.

**Clients include:** ChatWalrus (USA) · RE/MAX UAE · Protect Fortunes (USA) · and 45+ more across real estate, SaaS, e-commerce, and professional services.

## 🔧 &nbsp; What I Build

| System | What It Does |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **AI CMO Systems** | End-to-end marketing ops — content, outreach, and nurture — running without a human in the loop |
| 🤖 **Agentic Sales Pipelines** | AI agents that qualify leads, follow up autonomously, and book meetings across email, SMS, and CRM |
| 🎙️ **Voice AI Agents** | Outbound & inbound voice systems built on Vapi / Bland AI — for sales calls, appointment setting, support |
| 🔍 **RAG Systems** | Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines — knowledge bases, document Q&A, context-aware AI assistants |
| 📥 **Lead Generation Engines** | Scrapers, enrichers, and multi-step outbound sequences wired into your CRM — fully automated, always-on |
| 💬 **AI Customer Support** | Context-aware support agents trained on your product — integrated into helpdesk, WhatsApp, or web chat |
| ⚙️ **Workflow Automation** | Replace manual internal processes — reporting, data sync, task routing — with deterministic AI workflows |
| 🔗 **MCP Integrations** | Model Context Protocol-based agent tools connecting LLMs to live business data and external APIs |
